Delete your Yutaka account

You can permanently delete a self-hosted Yutaka sync account here without installing the Yutaka app.

Your credentials never pass through this page. Enter only your Yutaka Sync Worker URL. You will continue to the deletion form hosted by your own Worker, where you enter your username, password, and the required DELETE confirmation.
Use the same HTTPS Worker URL configured in Yutaka. If you paste a Worker page such as /profile, Yutaka will still open the correct deletion page.

What Worker-side deletion removes

  • the sync account and password verifier;
  • synchronized finance data and change history;
  • profile media;
  • registered devices, access/refresh sessions, and recovery credentials;
  • backup and Analytics schedules; and
  • stored Telegram and Google Drive configuration for that account.

What is not removed automatically

Local/offline copies already stored on your devices and files previously exported to Telegram or Google Drive remain until you delete them separately.

Other deletion option

If Yutaka is still installed and you are signed in, use Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
