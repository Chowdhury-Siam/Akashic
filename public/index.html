05 / THE DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Do I need to create an account? +

No. Yutaka works offline with locally stored data. A sync account is only needed when you choose to connect your own self-hosted backend.

Is the interactive demo the full app? +

No. It recreates selected Yutaka workflows using fictional data in your browser so you can explore the experience. It does not include live cloud sync, background notifications, or all native-device features.

How does multi-device sync work? +

Deploy your own Cloudflare Worker-based sync service, then connect devices to that Worker and sign in to the same sync account. There is no shared default cloud service.

Which platforms does Yutaka support? +

The app targets Android, Windows, Linux, and macOS. Play Store and Microsoft Store availability depends on the official listings being published.

Can I export reports and make backups? +

Yes. Yutaka supports local encrypted backup files and analytics exports in PDF, XLSX, and TXT. Optional Telegram and Google Drive delivery is configured through your own backend.
